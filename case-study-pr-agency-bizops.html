Case Study 04 / International Sport PR Agency

From relationship bottleneck to automated proposal engine.

I built an NDA-safe business development operating system for a global sport PR agency, connecting qualification, a custom Risk & Vulnerability Matrix, a custom Airtable CRM, and automated proposal generation.

Airtable CRM n8n iPhone Shortcuts Risk Matrix Proposal Automation
Narrative

Growth needed an operating system.

A global sport PR agency had opportunity, relationships, and credibility, but the business development motion was trapped in manual tracking and institutional memory. High-trust outreach carried risk, and proposal preparation took days.

I designed the system layer: structured qualification, a custom Risk & Vulnerability Matrix, Airtable CRM integration, and automated multilingual proposal generation. The outcome was speed with control: proposals moved from days to hours without adding headcount.
