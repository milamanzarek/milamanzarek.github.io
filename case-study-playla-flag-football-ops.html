Case Study 03 / PlayLA Flag Football Ops

Independent delivery of a citywide operations map for the City of Los Angeles.

As part of a major municipal partnership with the City of Los Angeles, I independently built a sport-agnostic relational database that connected schools, parks, active sites, and field operations so PlayLA could scale to a usable system view.

Airtable Google Cloud Mermaid.js Vite / TypeScript Bilingual Field App
Narrative

Building citywide infrastructure independently.

PlayLA expanded across Los Angeles in partnership with the City of Los Angeles, but the operating layer was still fragmented: isolated spreadsheets, site-by-site coordination, and critical details like coach certifications and school logistics living in too many places.

Working completely independently, I designed and shipped the relational system underneath the entire program: 1,520 schools, 419 parks, and active sites linked into a reusable model that could surface coverage gaps, support outreach, and make citywide municipal scale visible.
