## The challenge

My onboarding as Chair was one sentence: "Congratulations, you inherited a mess." The committee process carried real institutional weight, but the operating layer around it was too manual for a sensitive national Hall of Fame election.

## What I did

* Led the governance process itself: criteria, rubrics, and review decisions consolidated into one structured public system.
* Built the system behind it: a data model, database-backed scoring rules, a committee review portal, and an automated ballot and reporting pipeline.

## Results

* A live, transparent election cycle run on the new system.
* Quarterly board reporting delivered on schedule (most recent: June 2026).
* Governance that survives committee turnover because it lives in the system, not in one person's memory.

**Stack:** Neon (Postgres), Node.js pipeline, Netlify portal, Mermaid data model.
