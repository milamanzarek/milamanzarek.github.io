## The question

Fencing is Olympic, and still niche. VR research in sport had focused almost entirely on training skills; there was no data on whether VR could help promote a sport to new audiences, especially young people.

## What I did

* Designed and led an experimental study using real headsets (Meta Quest 2) and real participants.
* Ran the statistical analysis (SPSS and R) and published the results as a conference paper at the SMART SPORT & TOURISM conference within the KAZAN DIGITAL WEEK 2023 forum.

## Results

* Published experimental evidence on VR as an audience-development tool for a niche Olympic sport, with an honest read of what the data can and cannot say.
* A research foundation for my continuing work at the intersection of immersive technology and sport.

**Stack:** Meta Quest 2, experimental design, SPSS, R.
