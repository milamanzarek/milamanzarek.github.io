## The challenge

The client's operations were choking on manual workflows and disconnected scheduling systems: dispatchers double-booked technicians and appointments fell through the cracks.

## What I did

* Architected an enterprise diagnostic blueprint that mapped the operation end to end and prioritized the highest-leverage automations.
* Delivered automated scheduling integration (calendar-system integration across the dispatch workflow) and the surrounding process design.
* Packaged delivery as fixed-price modular sprints ($5K to $15K), so the client bought outcomes, not hours.

## Results

* Scheduling moved from manual coordination to automated integration.
* A staged, priced roadmap the client could execute one sprint at a time.

**Stack:** Airtable, Make/n8n, iCal scheduling integration.
