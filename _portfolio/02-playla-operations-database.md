## The challenge

A citywide youth sports program was expanding across Los Angeles, but the operating layer was fragmented: isolated spreadsheets, site-by-site coordination, and critical details like coach certifications and school logistics living in too many places.

## What I did

* Independently designed and built a sport-agnostic relational database connecting schools, parks, active sites, and field operations.
* Mapped 1,700+ facilities into one structured foundation, with relational logic linking feeder schools to nearby recreation centers to expose coverage gaps.
* Built the bilingual field companion tooling that let site staff work from the same data.

## Results

* One authoritative system view for a citywide municipal program, replacing fragmented spreadsheets.
* 15 active program sites operating from shared, structured location data.
* The data foundation was strong enough to power a second public-facing product (see the PlayLA public web app case).

**Stack:** Airtable, Google Cloud, Mermaid.js.
