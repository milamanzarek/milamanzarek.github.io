## The challenge

Nonprofits run on passion, but passion does not satisfy the IRS. A 501(c)(3) needs proper governance frameworks, digital compliance, and web infrastructure that protects the organization's liability while amplifying its mission, and this one had to get there on a shoestring budget.

## What I did

* Built the organization's web presence from scratch (live at tuganlyq.org).
* Instituted governance and compliance frameworks appropriate for a federally recognized 501(c)(3): documentation, compliance structure, and digital strategy.

## Results

* A secure, compliant digital footprint delivered on a volunteer budget.
* Governance treated as the license to operate, not overhead: liability protected, mission amplified.

**Stack:** Web development, nonprofit governance documentation, compliance structure.
