## The challenge

Grassroots sports organizations run on thin margins with no budget for high-end operational software. Coaches double as administrators, scheduling lives in group chats, and revenue tracking happens in personal spreadsheets.

## What I did

* Implemented the club operations stack for a youth tennis academy of about 80 athletes: CRM, scheduling, billing, and parent communication.
* Co-authored formal research on the engagement, published as a conference case study on AI-driven optimization of business operations for grassroots sports organizations.

## Results

As reported in the published case study:

* Over 40% reduction in administrator time spent on scheduling and booking.
* 23% increase in family response rates.
* A documented, repeatable model for AI-era operations at grassroots scale.

**Stack:** GoHighLevel, Stripe, Airtable, n8n.
