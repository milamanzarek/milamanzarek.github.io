## The challenge

A global sport PR agency had opportunity, relationships, and credibility, but the business development motion was trapped in manual tracking and institutional memory. High-trust outreach carried real reputational risk, and proposal preparation took days.

## What I did

* Designed the system layer in a 90-day engagement: structured qualification, a custom risk and vulnerability matrix, and a custom Airtable CRM, all NDA-safe.
* Automated multilingual proposal generation on top of the qualification data.

## Results

* Proposal preparation cut from days to hours.
* Business development knowledge moved out of individual memory into a system the whole agency operates.

**Stack:** Airtable CRM, workflow automation, iPhone Shortcuts, proposal automation.
