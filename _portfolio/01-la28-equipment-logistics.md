## The challenge

More than a million dollars of sports equipment sat scattered across Los Angeles warehouses with no central tracker: inventory state, value, and distribution context lived in fragmented places. In parallel, the LA28 Celebrates activation program needed end-to-end ownership across 150+ venue locations.

## What I did

* Audited and centralized the full inventory, then built the operating view on the organization's existing stack: Smartsheet, Workday, Salesforce, and Agiloft.
* Designed the distribution pipeline covering value, locations, quarterly distribution, and donation progress, and personally executed the physical logistics.
* Owned activations end to end: strategy, budgets, planning, execution, and post-event reporting across events, partnerships, technology, and leadership teams.

## Results

* 15% storage cost reduction on the equipment program.
* $300,000 of equipment delivered to LA youth programs in 2024.
* 100% process compliance, zero inventory loss.
* A cost-effective activation model across 150+ locations that deepened local-government and partner relationships.

**Stack:** Smartsheet, Workday, Salesforce, Agiloft.
