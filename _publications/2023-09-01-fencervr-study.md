I led an experimental study on virtual reality as a tool for attracting new audiences to fencing: real headsets, real participants, and an honest read of what the data can and cannot say. VR research in sport had focused almost entirely on training skills; this study addressed sport promotion, especially among young audiences. Methods: experimental design, Meta Quest 2 hardware, statistical analysis in SPSS and R.
