A formal case study of the operations stack I implemented for a youth tennis academy of about 80 athletes: CRM, scheduling, billing, and parent communication. Methods reported in the paper include case study, document analysis, interviews, observation, and quantitative analysis. Reported results: more than 40% less administrator time for scheduling and booking, and a 23% increase in response rates.
