Case Study 25 / AiSA Generator App

From fragmented collateral to a locked-down brand engine.

I built an internal React/TypeScript application integrating the Gemini 2.5 Flash API that embeds brand constraints into the production surface. It instantly turns a single concept into 130KB of platform-ready marketing copy, presentations, and visual assets without breaking brand guidelines.

React & TypeScript Google Gemini 2.5 API Firebase Auth & Hosting
Strategic Frame

Brand governance works better when the guardrails live inside the tool.

AiSA needed repeatable, branded marketing assets: course slides, certificates, social posts, carousels, covers, QR grids, and campaign copy. The operational pain was not only production speed. It was the recurring cost of reviewing, correcting, and policing work that should have been on brand from the start.

The generator app changes the job from micromanaging output to designing the conditions for good output. Team members can choose an approved format, theme, layout, topic, and image slot, while the app limits the ways the brand can be bent out of shape.
