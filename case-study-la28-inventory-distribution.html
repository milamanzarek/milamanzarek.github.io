Case Study 02 / LA28

From scattered inventory to a dashboard-backed distribution system.

I built a Smartsheet-based inventory dashboard to track value, locations, quarterly distribution, and donation progress for sports equipment moving across Los Angeles.

Smartsheet Dashboard Workday Salesforce Agiloft
Narrative

One person, scattered assets, no central tracker.

The starting point was not a messy dashboard. It was no dashboard at all: more than a million dollars in sports equipment spread across Los Angeles warehouses, with inventory state, value, and distribution context living in fragmented places.

I turned that into a practical operating view using the original stack: Smartsheet, Workday, Salesforce, and Agiloft. The dashboard made inventory value, donation value, location notes, and distribution reports visible enough to act on.
