Case Study 07 / PlayLA Public Webapp

Bilingual municipal public webapp shipped independently for the City of Los Angeles.

In partnership with the City of Los Angeles, I independently built and shipped a mobile-first public resource that reused the database foundation to surface active program locations with direct Google Maps directions loaded for families.

Public Resource Portal Location Data Reuse Parent Logistics Bilingual UX Community Trust Mobile-First Build
Narrative

One data foundation, multiple jobs.

In the operations phase of this municipal program for the City of Los Angeles, I built the underlying location data model. Working completely independently, I reused that structured foundation of 1,700+ mapped facilities to design, develop, and ship a bilingual public webapp for local families.

The public webapp turns this database into location search, interactive program cards, rules, parent guidelines, equipment lists, and emergency resources. To make the interface highly actionable, I did not just list static address strings. Clicking the program card launches Google Maps with active directions to the specific recreation center already loaded, reducing parent coordination friction to a single tap.

Workflow

How one database served multiple purposes.

01

Map the city

The PlayLA Flag Football Ops database structured a 1,700+ location foundation with addresses, linked schools, and geocoordinates.

02

Reuse the data

The app reused that foundation to surface the 15 currently active Flag Football program locations to parents.

03

Load directions

Location cards move directly into Google Maps directions so parent logistics do not depend on copy-paste, vague park names, or extra taps.

04

Extend the portal

The app also packaged rules, parent guidance, bilingual equipment info, emergency support, and partner context.
