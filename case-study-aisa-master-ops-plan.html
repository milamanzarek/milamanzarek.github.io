Case Study 08 / Strategic Leadership

Building the operating system before the launch.

I built the AI Start Academy Master Operations Plan as a COO-level command center: governance, RACI, operating cadence, OKRs, roadmap, SOP registry, KPI dashboard, and launch readiness in one bootstrapped Google Sheets system.

Executive Operations RACI Governance OKRs SOP Registry KPI Dashboard Google Sheets OS
Narrative

A strategic plan that could actually run the company.

This was not a decorative strategy deck. It was a centralized operational framework for launching, scaling, and managing an AI-focused education ecosystem with clear governance, timelines, ownership, and performance discipline.

Because the startup was bootstrapped, I deliberately avoided expensive enterprise tooling and built the operating logic in a structured spreadsheet. The point was leadership clarity: one place where executives, functional leads, and execution teams could see what matters, who owns it, and what happens next.

Audience

Founder team, COO/CPO/CEO functions, operations, marketing, education, IT, and delivery teams.

Artifact

A master operations plan with launch phases, accountability, cadence, roadmap, SOPs, and metrics.

Method

Zero-cost spreadsheet architecture, grouped execution layers, governance logic, and KPI discipline.

Signal

Strategic leadership: aligning vision, people, priorities, systems, and measurable execution.

Leadership System

The operating architecture behind the spreadsheet.

Governance and decision rights

RACI logic connects deliverables to CEO, CPO, COO, marketing, education, and technical functions so ownership is visible.

Operating rhythm

Weekly execution reviews, monthly plan reviews, and strategic pivot-or-persevere reviews keep leadership decisions moving.

Launch phases

Launch and validate, scale and optimize, then maturity: a phased progression model that keeps ambition sequenced.

Human capital planning

Team growth is modeled from lean pre-launch staffing into scale-stage capacity instead of hiring from panic.

Roadmap and dependencies

Q1 milestones and a companion work breakdown/Gantt view translate strategy into actual sequencing.

KPI and SOP discipline

Metrics and process standardization are built into the operating model instead of bolted on after launch.
